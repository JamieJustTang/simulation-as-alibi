# Stream 2 — Platform Documentation (5 commercial platforms)

As declared in the paper (Section 3.2, Data Streams): *"Stream 2 comprises
documentation from five commercial platforms (Artificial Societies, Altera,
Character.AI, Voyager, Google DeepMind)."*

| # | Platform | Role in the paper |
|---|----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Artificial Societies | Primary commercial case (Case A); documentation analyzed for interaction topology, action grammar, sanction architecture, beneficiary structure |
| 2 | Altera | Background commercial context (digital humans, $42M raise) |
| 3 | Character.AI | Background commercial context (massive daily agent interactions) |
| 4 | Voyager | Background commercial context (agent societies platform) |
| 5 | Google DeepMind | Background commercial context (agent research) |

Case A (Artificial Societies) is the only platform analyzed in depth; the
other four platforms provide background context on the scale and trajectory
of commercial deployment, as stated in Section 6 of the paper. All materials
analyzed are publicly available documents; the case studies are document-based
pattern inference, as stated in the paper.
